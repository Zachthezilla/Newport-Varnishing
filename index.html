The Newport Standard

Preserving Maritime Heritage

Newport is home to some of the world's most significant classic yachts. We understand that brightwork on these vessels is more than aesthetic—it's a preservation of history. Our methods respect traditional craftsmanship while utilizing modern UV protection tech.

The Atlantic Climate Challenge

The Rhode Island coast is unforgiving. High humidity, salt-saturated air, and intense summer UV rays can cause varnish to amber and crack in a single season. Our multi-coat systems are engineered specifically to thrive in this environment.

Structural Teak Integrity

Teak decks and rails are a massive investment. Improper sanding can strip away years of life from the wood. We use precision depth-control techniques to ensure we only remove the grayed surface, leaving your structural teak thick and strong.

The 12-Coat Process

01. Preparation & Stripping

Hardware is carefully removed to prevent water intrusion points. Old finishes are stripped to reveal the wood's true character and natural beauty.

02. Precision Hand-Sanding

Multiple stages of hand-sanding ensure a perfectly level surface. We eliminate grain inconsistencies to create a uniform foundation for the sealer.

03. Varnish Build-Up

A traditional build-up of coats creates the necessary depth and protection. This labor-intensive phase is what gives our work its legendary optical clarity.

04. The Final Polish

Ultra-fine grit wet sanding and final flow coats create a glass-like finish that stands up to the closest inspection at the dock.

Marine FAQ

What is the benefit of a maintenance coat?

Maintenance coats act as a sacrificial layer. By adding fresh varnish annually, you replace the UV inhibitors that the sun destroys, preventing the need for costly full stripping.

Which marinas do you serve?

We provide mobile services across Newport Harbor, Safe Harbor NEB, Jamestown Boat Yard, and all major Narragansett Bay facilities.

How long does a full project take?

Timing depends on weather and the number of coats. A full restoration usually takes 2-4 weeks to allow for proper curing between applications.

Can you work on interior wood?

Yes. We apply the same level of precision to galleys, saloons, and companionways, often using specific low-VOC or satin finishes for cabin comfort.
